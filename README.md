NOTICE: please check your local laws and regulations about hobbyist radio transmissions and HAM radio lisences. You may be fined or even put into jail
This uses an ESP32 main board for all computing and a CDEBYTE E22-900M33S SPI LoRa module for voice and text transmission. You are able to connect with your phone to the esp and send and recieve texts from your phone
